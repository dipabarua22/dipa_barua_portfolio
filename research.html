Capstone Research Project

A Privacy-Preserving, Real-Time Emergency Hand Gesture Recognition Framework Using Deep Learning and MediaPipe Landmarks for Women’s Safety

Ongoing

Research Overview

This research focuses on developing a real-time women’s safety system that recognizes predefined emergency hand gestures using smartphone cameras, MediaPipe hand landmarks, and lightweight deep learning models.

The system is designed to operate directly on Android devices without depending on cloud-based image processing. When a distress gesture is detected, the application can automatically trigger emergency actions such as SOS messages, emergency calls, and live GPS location sharing with predefined contacts.

Research Goal

The primary goal is to create a privacy-focused and reliable emergency gesture recognition framework that can perform in real-world conditions including low light, motion blur, occlusion, different camera angles, and varied user behavior.

Key Features

Emergency Gesture Recognition

Detects predefined distress gestures through smartphone camera input.

On-Device Processing

Performs gesture recognition locally for improved privacy and low-latency response.

Emergency Response

Supports SOS messaging, emergency calls, and live GPS location sharing.

Privacy-Preserving Design

Processes necessary gesture information without storing or transmitting raw images or videos.
